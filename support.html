Common questions

I deleted photos by accident. Can I get them back?

Yes — and this is the important part. Swipeshine never deletes anything permanently. Deleted photos go to your iPhone's own Recently Deleted album, where they stay recoverable for 30 days. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted, select the photos, and tap Recover.

Where are the photos I swiped left on?

They collect in Swipeshine's Trash, which you review before anything is actually deleted. Nothing is removed from your library until you tap Confirm on that screen.

What does the free version include?

40 photos, free, once. That's two complete rounds of review-and-confirm. If you want to clean more, there's a single one-time purchase — no subscription, ever.

I bought the unlock on another device. How do I restore it?

Settings (the gear icon) → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Swipeshine upload my photos?

No. There is no server. Everything — displaying, estimating sizes, deleting — happens on your iPhone. The app has no analytics and no tracking. See our privacy policy.

Some photos say they can't be deleted. Why?

Photos synced to your iPhone from a computer (via Finder or iTunes) can't be deleted by any app, including this one — iOS doesn't allow it. Swipeshine skips them and tells you how many it skipped on the results screen. Delete those from the computer that synced them, or from iCloud.com.

The app says I need to allow photo access.

Swipeshine can't work without it — it's a photo review tool. Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Swipeshine and choose All Photos (or Limited Access, which also works — you'll pick which photos Swipeshine can see).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Airplane mode is fine. If you have iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage" turned on, some full-resolution originals may need to download first; there's a "Wi-Fi only" switch in Settings if you want to avoid using cellular data.
