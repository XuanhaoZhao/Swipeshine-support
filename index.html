PySpire Support

PySpire is a Python coding app for iPad — a complete Python interpreter that runs entirely on your device. No account, no internet connection required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PySpire really free?
Yes. You get 12 free runs every day. Editing, saving, opening files, and the built-in sample scripts are free forever. If you need more than 12 runs in one day, unlock unlimited runs with a one-time purchase — no subscription, no ads.

Does my code leave my device?
No. PySpire never connects to the internet. All code runs locally on your iPad.

Where are my scripts saved?
In the Files app under On My iPad → PySpire. You can also open any .py file from the Files app with PySpire.

I bought Unlimited but it's not working.
Tap Restore on the purchase screen, or tap the remaining-quota badge and choose Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Family Sharing members may need to restore on their own device.

Which iPad do I need?
iPadOS 17.5 or later.

How do I stop a script that runs forever?
Tap Stop (or ⌘. on a hardware keyboard). Infinite loops are also stopped automatically after a time limit.
