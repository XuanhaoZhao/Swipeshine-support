Swipeshine

Swipe to clean your photos.

Swipe left to delete, right to keep. The ones you delete wait in a Trash you review before anything actually goes — and even then, iOS keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Why it's different

Nothing is final

You see every photo before it's deleted, and you confirm the whole batch at once. Swiped-left photos collect in a Trash — nothing leaves your library until you tap Confirm on that screen. After that, they're still recoverable from the iPhone's own Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

Nothing leaves your iPhone

No server, no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising. Your photos are never uploaded — there is nowhere for them to go. The app works in airplane mode.

No subscription

40 photos free, once. After that it's a single one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited cleanup. Nothing auto-renews, ever. Restore it on any device with the same Apple Account.
