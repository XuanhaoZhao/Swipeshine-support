Swipeshine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version: Swipeshine does not collect your data. There is no server, no account, no analytics, and no tracking. Your photos never leave your device.

What the app accesses, and why

Your photo library

Swipeshine asks for photo library access for exactly two reasons: to display your photos so you can review them, and to delete the ones you choose. This access is required for the app to function at all — it is a photo review tool.

All image processing happens on your device. Swipeshine has no servers, so there is nowhere for your photos to be sent even in principle. The app does not upload your photos, thumbnails, metadata, or anything derived from them.

Storage space figures

To show you how much space a deletion would free, Swipeshine estimates the size of each photo from its pixel dimensions and format. This calculation happens entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Specifically, Swipeshine does not collect:

  • Your photos, videos, or anything derived from them
  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or any other identifier
  • Location data
  • Usage statistics, analytics, or crash reports
  • Any information about how you use the app

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no analytics libraries. It makes no network requests to us.

In-app purchase

Swipeshine offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited photo cleanup. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, name, or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

To know whether you have already purchased the unlock, the app reads your App Store purchase entitlement from the device. This stays on your device.

The free quota

Swipeshine's free tier (40 photos) is tracked in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is not sent anywhere. We have no way to know how many photos you have cleaned.

Children

Swipeshine does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control

You can revoke Swipeshine's photo library access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Swipeshine. The app will stop being able to show or delete photos, and no data of yours is retained by us because we never had any.

Deleting the app removes all of its on-device data. The free-quota counter is stored in the Keychain, which iOS retains after uninstalling an app; if you reinstall Swipeshine, your remaining free quota is restored rather than reset. You can clear it by erasing your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Since the app collects no data, we do not anticipate changes.
