PySpire Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

PySpire is designed to be completely offline. This policy explains what little data there is to explain.

Data we collect

None. PySpire does not create an account, does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and does not make any network requests. Your code and your scripts stay on your iPad and are never sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

PySpire offers a one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; PySpire receives no payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchase processing.

Analytics and advertising

PySpire contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Children

Because PySpire collects no data of any kind, it is safe for learners of all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this page.
